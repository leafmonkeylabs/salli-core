# Business notes

How Leaf Monkey Labs means to make Salli pay for itself. These are working
notes for investors, design partners and the team, not product
documentation: nothing here is built into Salli, and the product's code and
docs stay free of any one country and of plans and billing (CLAUDE.md).

- [thesis.md](thesis.md): the commercial thesis for the first launch:
  practices as the wedge, a capped free hosted book, one charge per way of
  running Salli.
- [commercial-licence.md](commercial-licence.md): what is under which
  licence, the one-charge rule, and the offer for the AGPL-3.0 parts.
- [metrics.md](metrics.md): what we count, and how a server counts it
  without looking at anyone's money.

**Everything marked _Hypothesis_ is one**: a guess to test, not a result.
There are no customers, revenue or usage figures in these notes, because
there are none yet.
